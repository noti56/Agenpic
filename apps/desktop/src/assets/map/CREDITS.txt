Agenpic presence-map art
========================

characters/hero-01..15.png
agents/agent-01..09.png

	Cut from AI-generated sprite-sheet reference boards commissioned by the
	project owner (ChatGPT image generation, Aug 2026). Extracted with a
	background-matte + connected-component script: each sprite is the
	tightest bounding box of one character, alpha-matted against the board's
	panel colour and resampled to a uniform height (heroes 64px, agents
	56px, 2px transparent padding).

	The nine agent variants are the same robot in nine team colours. The
	colour each one actually carries is recorded as `accent` in
	src/components/sprites/heroDefs.ts — it is measured from the art, not
	taken from the captions printed on the source board, which do not all
	match the robot drawn beneath them.

	These are project-owned assets, not third-party CC0 art. Do not
	redistribute them as a stock sprite pack.
